### 🧑‍💻 About me

I'm a **full-stack developer** who builds the systems businesses run on: property management, restaurant platforms, CMSs, invoicing and payroll. I work across **Angular**, **Next.js** and **Node.js**, and I care about clean architecture, correct money math and interfaces people enjoy using.

- 💼 **Now:** Full-Stack Developer @ **Intuitive Solutions** · Jul 2025 – present
  - Built a Property Management System end to end with Angular 19, Ionic 8, Node.js and MongoDB
  - Designed and built invoice and payroll backends; migrated a CMS template from Angular 9 + Bootstrap 4 to Angular 20 + Tailwind
  - Wrote a Playwright tool that finds broken backlinks and checks Google Business Profiles
- 🎓 **B.Sc. in CSE**, East West University
- 🎯 **Focus:** multi-tenant SaaS, REST APIs, SSR and performance, design systems
- 🌐 **Languages:** English (IELTS 7.5), Bengali, Hindi/Urdu
